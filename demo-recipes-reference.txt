RECIPE 1: Spaghetti
------------------------------------------------------------
Title:       Spaghetti
Time:        30 Minutes
Tags:        Vegan, 4 ingredients, Easy
Description: A simple, comforting pasta with tomato sauce.
Image URL:   [NEEDS URL - was local file spaghetti.jpg]
             Suggestion: https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1598866594230-a7c12756260f?w=800

Ingredients (one per line):
8 oz spaghetti
2 cups tomato sauce
2 cloves garlic, minced
2 tbsp olive oil

Instructions (one per line):

RECIPE 2: Spaghetti Carbonara
------------------------------------------------------------
Title:       Spaghetti Carbonara
Time:        25 Minutes
Tags:        Pork, Creamy, Italian
Description: Classic carbonara with eggs, Parmesan, and crispy pancetta.
Image URL:   [NEEDS URL - was local file carbonara.jpg]
             Suggestion: https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1612874742237-6526221588e3?w=800

Ingredients (one per line):
8 oz spaghetti
2 eggs
1/2 cup grated Parmesan
4 oz pancetta or bacon
Salt & pepper

Instructions (one per line):
Cook pasta until al dente, reserving 1/2 cup of the pasta water.
Crisp pancetta in a pan and set aside.
Whisk eggs and Parmesan together in a bowl.
Toss hot pasta with pancetta, then quickly stir in egg mixture, using pasta water as needed to create a silky sauce.

RECIPE 3: Weeknight Chickpea Curry
------------------------------------------------------------
Title:       Weeknight Chickpea Curry
Time:        35 Minutes
Tags:        Vegetarian, Gluten-free, Spicy
Description: A cozy coconut chickpea curry for busy nights.
Image URL:   [NEEDS URL - was local file curry.jpg]
             Suggestion: https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1565557623262-b51c2513a641?w=800

Ingredients (one per line):
1 tbsp oil
1 onion, diced
2 tbsp curry paste
1 can chickpeas, drained
1 can coconut milk
Salt to taste

Instructions (one per line):
Sauté onion in oil until soft.
Stir in curry paste and cook for 1 minute.
Add chickpeas and coconut milk, then simmer for 15 minutes.
Serve over rice with your favorite toppings.

RECIPE 5: Chocolate Chip Cookies
------------------------------------------------------------
Title:       Chocolate Chip Cookies
Time:        30 Minutes
Tags:        Dessert
Description: Soft and chewy cookies loaded with warm chocolate chips.
Image URL:   [NEEDS URL - was local file cookie.jpg]
             Suggestion: https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1499636136210-6f4ee915583e?w=800

Ingredients (one per line):
2 1/4 cups flour
1 cup butter, softened
3/4 cup sugar
3/4 cup brown sugar
1 egg + 1 egg yolk
1 1/2 cups chocolate chips

Instructions (one per line):
Cream butter and sugars together until light and fluffy.
Beat in egg and egg yolk, then mix in dry ingredients until just combined.
Fold in chocolate chips.
Scoop onto baking sheet and bake at 350°F for 10–12 minutes.
